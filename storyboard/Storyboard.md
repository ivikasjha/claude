# Storyboard: Bridging the Gap: From Innovation to Patient Impact

*Ethics, Evidence, Regulation and Responsible Medical Device Translation*

**Presenter:** Dr Vikas Kumar Jha · KIIT University, Bhubaneswar  
**Length:** 35–40 minutes (≈35 min scripted + 3–5 min flexible discussion) (scripted total 35:00)

## Story spine

We follow two composite patients, Ramesh (Parkinson’s, freezing of gait, FoGO) and Kamala (knee osteoarthritis, SwaKnee), along a six-stage path: Need → Evidence → People → Permission → Access → Safety. Each stage answers one patient question. Every case claim is tagged Demonstrated, Reported (preliminary), Planned or Not established.

## Teaching lenses

Stanford Biodesign (need-led process, Principled Decision-Making) · Harvard bioethics (HMS Center for Bioethics, MRCT Center, Petrie-Flom) · Cambridge (Engineering Design Centre inclusive design, Engineering Better Care systems approach) → adapted to India (CDSCO MDR-2017, ICMR 2017, MvPI, NPPA, UCMPMD 2024, DPDP).

## Evidence-status key

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Demonstrated** | Shown directly (e.g., prototype function on a volunteer) |
| **Reported, preliminary** | Reported by the project or company; not independently verified or peer reviewed |
| **Planned** | Future work in the project plan; not a result |
| **Not established** | No adequate evidence yet; must not be claimed |
