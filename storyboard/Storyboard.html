Storyboard: Bridging the Gap: From Innovation to Patient Impact

Ethics, Evidence, Regulation and Responsible Medical Device Translation · Dr Vikas Kumar Jha · KIIT University, Bhubaneswar · 35–40 minutes (≈35 min scripted + 3–5 min flexible discussion); scripted total 35:00

Story spine. We follow two composite patients, Ramesh (Parkinson’s, freezing of gait, FoGO) and Kamala (knee osteoarthritis, SwaKnee), along a six-stage path: Need → Evidence → People → Permission → Access → Safety. Each stage answers one patient question. Every case claim is tagged Demonstrated, Reported (preliminary), Planned or Not established.
Teaching lenses. Stanford Biodesign (need-led process, Principled Decision-Making, Stanford-India Biodesign) · Harvard (HMS Center for Bioethics, MRCT Center, Petrie-Flom, HBS cases) · Cambridge (Engineering Design Centre Inclusive Design Toolkit, Engineering Better Care, Institute for Biomedical Innovation, Judge CCHLE) → adapted to India (CDSCO MDR-2017, ICMR 2017, MvPI, NPPA, UCMPMD 2024, DPDP Rules 2025).
