- **[confirmed] P2** NMDP 2023 rests on six strategies: (1) regulatory streamlining, (2) enabling infrastructure, (3) facilitating R&D and innovation, (4) attracting investments, (5) human resources development, (6) brand positioning and awareness creation, including an Export Promotion Council.

## PLI scheme design
- **[confirmed] P3** PLI Scheme for Medical Devices: outlay Rs 3,420 crore; tenure FY2020-21 to FY2027-28; 5% incentive on incremental sales for five years (FY2022-23 to FY2026-27); four target segments: cancer care/radiotherapy devices; radiology & imaging (ionising & non-ionising) and nuclear imaging devices; anaesthetics & cardio-respiratory devices; all implants including implantable electronic devices.  
  Source: PLI Scheme for Medical Devices: Unleashing the potential (Invest India) (2023) <https://investindia.gov.in/blogs/pli-scheme-medical-devices-unleashing-potential-make-india-make-world> — Note: Scheme administered by DoP with IFCI as PMA: https://plimedicaldevices.ifciltd.com/

## Licensing by class
- **[confirmed] R6** Under the Medical Devices Rules 2017, Class A and B manufacturing licences (Form MD-3 application, MD-5 licence) are issued by the State Licensing Authority; Class C and D (MD-7 application, MD-9 licence) by the Central Licensing Authority (CDSCO); import licences use MD-14 (application) and MD-15 (licence) for all classes.  
  Source: Types of medical device approval licenses issued by CDSCO in India (Artixio) (2024) <https://www.artixio.com/post/types-of-medical-device-approval-licenses-issued-by-cdsco-in-india> — Note: Consistent with Treelife: https://treelife.in/?p=16450 and Freyr.

## MvPI report volumes

## DePuy ASR case
- **[confirmed] V6** CDSCO issued a medical device alert on the DePuy ASR hip implant in December 2013 after about 4,700 surgeries in India; only ~882 patients were traceable; DePuy's import licence was cancelled under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940.  
  Source: Reassessing India's Medical Device Recall Framework: Regulatory Gaps and the Case for Reform (Mondaq) (2025) <https://www.mondaq.com/reassessing-india's-medical-device-recall-framework-regulatory-gaps-and-the-case-for-reform/1751304> — Note: Patient numbers corroborated by The Wire and The Quint reports (2018).

## DePuy ASR compensation
- **[confirmed] V7** In November 2018 the Health Ministry approved a compensation formula of Rs 30 lakh to Rs 1.23 crore per ASR patient (base Rs 20 lakh plus Rs 10 lakh non-pecuniary damages, adjusted for age and disability), following the Agarwal Committee report (Aug 2018) and the Dr R.K. Arya central expert committee (Oct 2018); CDSCO later directed J&J to pay, e.g. over Rs 2 crore for two further patients.  
  Source: Centre approves Rs 30L to Rs 1.23 cr relief in faulty hip implants case (Hindustan Times via PressReader) (2018-11-30) <https://www.pressreader.com/india/hindustan-times-delhi/20181130/281852939633326> — Note: Also https://business.medicaldialogues.in/jnj-hip-implants-cdsco-orders-compensation-of-over-rs-2-crore-for-2-more-patients and https://science.thewire.in/health/victims-of-jj-faulty-hip-implants-seek-review-of-proposed-compensation-formula/

## NPPA powers
- **[confirmed] N1** Medical devices are 'drugs' under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940, so NPPA regulates them under DPCO 2013: Para 19 allows fixing ceiling prices of non-scheduled items in extraordinary circumstances/public interest; Para 20 requires NPPA to monitor MRPs of non-scheduled formulations (including devices) so that no MRP rises more than 10% in any preceding 12 months.  
  Source: NPPA asks for Price Movement details of Orthopedic Implants (Medical Dialogues) (2017) <https://medicaldialogues.in/nppa-asks-for-price-movement-details-of-orthopedic-implants> — Note: Para 20 mechanics also in Cyril Amarchand analysis of Bharat Serums v UoI (Delhi HC 2023), which rejected NPPA's interpretation of para 20 in that drug case: https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2023/03/analysing-bharat-serums-and-vaccines-limited-v-union-of-india-ors-judgement/ . Para 19 as the basis for the 2017 stent/knee caps is from memory.
